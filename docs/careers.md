- Environment & Agriculture: 3

**Skill / Aptitude Alignment:**

- Problem Solving: 5
- Verbal Ability: 4

#### 2. Academic Relevance
- **Physics:** High

- **Biology:** High
- **Mathematics:** High

#### 3. Pathways & Requirements

2. Prepare for IISc/IISER
3. Seek summer research fellowships
