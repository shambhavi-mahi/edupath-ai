# EduPath — Problem Statement



## Problem



Students often struggle to choose suitable academic streams and

career paths because career guidance is often generic and does not

adequately consider their individual academic performance, aptitude,

interests, skills, personality and goals.



## Solution



EduPath is an AI-powered career exploration platform that analyzes

a student's academic profile, aptitude, interests and skills to

identify relevant career paths and provide an actionable roadmap.



## Core Value Proposition



EduPath helps students answer:



1. What are my strengths?

2. Which career paths may fit my profile?

3. Why are these paths recommended?

4. What skills do I need to develop?

5. What should I do next?
