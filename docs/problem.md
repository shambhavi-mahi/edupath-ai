# Problem Statement

## Overview

Students in India — especially those completing Class 10 — face one of the most critical decisions of their academic life: **choosing the right stream**. This choice cascades into career direction, entrance exam preparation, college selection, and ultimately, livelihood.

## The Problem

Existing career guidance is:

- **Generic** — one-size-fits-all advice that ignores individual differences
- **Inaccessible** — quality counsellors are expensive and concentrated in urban areas
- **Reactive** — students seek help only after making a wrong choice
- **Disconnected** — stream advice, exam guidance, and college selection are siloed

Students struggle to align:
- Their **strengths and aptitude** (logical, creative, scientific, verbal, etc.)
- Their **interests** (what they enjoy doing)
- Their **academic performance** (realistic assessment of current ability)
- Their **goals** (income, impact, lifestyle, location)
- **Market realities** (which careers have future demand)

## Impact

- Many students end up in streams/courses they dislike or are unsuited for
- Dropout rates increase in engineering and medical streams
- Potential talent goes unrealised due to lack of awareness of alternative paths
- First-generation learners are most severely affected

## Opportunity

An AI-powered, personalised guidance system that:
1. Assesses the student holistically (aptitude, interests, performance, goals)
2. Maps them to suitable streams, careers, and exams
3. Provides a step-by-step, actionable education roadmap
4. Is accessible 24/7, free, and available in regional contexts
