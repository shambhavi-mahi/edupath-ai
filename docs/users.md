# Target Users

## Primary Users

### 1. Class 10 Students (14–16 years)
- At the stream selection crossroads (Science / Commerce / Arts / Vocational)
- Uncertain about aptitude and long-term direction
- Influenced by family pressure and peer choices
- Need: Stream recommendation + career overview + exam roadmap

### 2. Class 8–9 Students (13–15 years)
- Early awareness seekers
- Want to know what to prepare for
- Need: Career exploration + subject guidance

### 3. Class 11–12 Students (16–18 years)
- Already in a stream, now choosing college and entrance exams
- Need: Exam guidance + college recommendations + backup plans

## Secondary Users

### 4. College Students / Graduates (18–24 years)
- Exploring specialisations, job roles, or career pivots
- Need: Job role finder + skill roadmap

### 5. Parents
- Want to understand options available to their child
- Seek validated, data-backed guidance
- Need: Simple, trustworthy summary of recommendations

## User Personas

### Priya — Class 10 student, Tier-2 city
- Good at Math, unsure about Science vs Commerce
- Parents want her to be a doctor but she enjoys design
- Needs unbiased, personalised guidance

### Arjun — Class 12 PCM, JEE aspirant
- Struggling with JEE prep, considering alternatives
- Needs realistic alternative career paths + exams

### Meera — B.Sc. graduate, job seeker
- Completed Biology but unsure of career options beyond medicine
- Needs job role discovery + skill gap analysis

## Accessibility Goals

- Works on low-end Android devices
- Available in English (Hindi/regional language support planned)
- No login required for basic assessment
- Free tier covers core functionality
